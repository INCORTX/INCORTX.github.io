A · Tasks & metrics

A box is four numbers — how close is close?

two boxes as (x₁, y₁, x₂, y₂) — their overlap is a box too

Session 7: a box is four numbers in pixel coordinates — where on the image, not how far away (session 2).

The overlap starts at the larger left edge and ends at the smaller right edge (same for top and bottom; 0 if they miss):

$$\text{IoU}=\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}=\frac{I}{|A|+|B|-I}$$

1 = the same box · 0 = no overlap. A detection counts as a hit when IoU ≥ 0.5.

Worked example · the picture

I = (5 − 3)·(4 − 2) = 4 · |A| = 4·3 = 12 · |B| = 4·4 = 16 · IoU = 4 ÷ (12 + 16 − 4) = 4/24 ≈ 0.17 — a miss

🇹🇭 ที่มา IoU: ส่วนซ้อนเริ่มที่ขอบซ้ายที่มากกว่า จบที่ขอบขวาที่น้อยกว่า · union = A + B − I (ไม่นับส่วนซ้อนสองครั้ง) · ย้อนคาบ 7 (กล่อง = พิกัด) และคาบ 2 (บอกตำแหน่งบนภาพ ไม่ใช่ระยะ) · ตัวอย่างไม่ซ้ำ Try it หน้า 11

🇬🇧 Where IoU comes from: the overlap runs from the larger left edge to the smaller right edge; union = A + B − I (do not count the overlap twice). Back to session 7 (a box is coordinates) and session 2 (where on the image, not how far). The example differs from slide 11's Try it.

A · Tasks & metrics

Precision and recall pull against each other

$$P=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}\qquad R=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}$$

Precision: of the boxes, how many are right. Recall: of the people, how many were found.

Raise the threshold: fewer, surer boxes — P up, R down. The area under the whole curve is average precision (AP = 0.76 here); mAP averages it over classes.

Session 4, slide 19: measure on a test set the model never saw — and the same data for every model you compare.

🇹🇭 ลาก threshold: 0.25 → P 7/11 = 0.64 · R 7/12 = 0.58 · 0.5 → P 4/4 = 1.00 · R 4/12 = 0.33 · AP = พื้นที่ใต้เส้น 0.76 (นับแบบ all-point) · ย้อนคาบ 4 หน้า 19 (test set) · เกณฑ์ Demo ข้อ 1 ของโปรเจกต์

🇬🇧 Drag the threshold: 0.25 → P 7/11 = 0.64, R 7/12 = 0.58; 0.5 → P 4/4 = 1.00, R 4/12 = 0.33. AP = the area under the curve, 0.76 (all-point). Back to session 4 slide 19 (test set) and the project's Demo criterion 1.

A · Tasks & metrics

Non-maximum suppression: one box per object

Before NMS, YOLOv8n gives 62 person boxes with score ≥ 0.25 — several on each cyclist.

  1. take the highest-scoring box left — keep it;
  2. remove every box with IoU > 0.5 against it;
  3. repeat until none are left.

🇹🇭 กด One step ทีละครั้ง: กล่องคะแนนสูงสุด (ขอบหนา) ถูกเก็บ กล่องที่ซ้อน IoU > 0.5 หายไป · สุดท้ายเหลือ 8 จาก 62 (ตรงกับ torchvision.ops.nms) · NMS ของ YOLO เองใช้ 0.7 จึงเหลือกล่องซ้ำบางกล่อง (FP หน้า 7)

🇬🇧 Press One step: the best box (thick) is kept, the boxes overlapping it with IoU > 0.5 disappear. 8 of 62 remain (the same as torchvision.ops.nms). YOLO's own NMS uses 0.7, which is why some second boxes survive (slide 7's FP).

Which metric fits your project?

Missing is costly → recallA tumour screen, a person on a level crossing: a lower threshold, and a human checks the extra boxes.
False alarms are costly → precisionAn automatic fine, a door that locks: a higher threshold, and accept some misses.
Counting → per-image count errorCars in a car park, birds on a wire: whether the number is right matters more than each box.
Exact outline → mask IoU (part C)Measuring a leaf or a wound: boxes are not enough.

Project Demo, criterion 1: “a metric that fits the problem; the same data for every solution”. Say which mistake hurts more — then choose the metric.

C · Segmentation

Shrink to understand, grow back to point

sizes in pixels per side · an encoder–decoder (the U-Net shape)

Session 4, slide 31: a transposed convolution grows a map — out = (n − 1)s − 2p + k.

The encoder (a CNN, stride 2 again and again) trades size for meaning. The decoder grows it back with transposed convolutions:

With k = 2, s = 2, p = 0, every step doubles:

$$\text{out}=(n-1)\cdot 2-0+2=2n$$
Worked example · a 384 × 384 photo, encoder ÷ 8

384 → 192 → 96 → 48 · decoder: 48 → 96 → 192 → 384 — three transposed convolutions · SAM (measured): 1024 → 64 × 64, ÷ 16

🇹🇭 ที่มา: encoder ÷2 หลายครั้ง (ได้ความหมาย เสียตำแหน่ง) → decoder ×2 ด้วย transposed conv · สูตรคาบ 4 หน้า 31 กับ k 2 s 2 p 0 ได้ 2n พอดี · ตัวอย่าง 384 ÷ 8 ไม่ซ้ำ Try it หน้า 27 · SAM จริง 1024 → 64

🇬🇧 Where it comes from: the encoder halves again and again (meaning up, position down); the decoder doubles with transposed convolutions. Session 4 slide 31's formula with k 2, s 2, p 0 gives exactly 2n. The 384 ÷ 8 example differs from slide 27's Try it. SAM, measured: 1024 → 64.

C · Segmentation

Skip connections bring back the fine detail

Session 4 D: a skip connection carries a layer's input past the layer — y = F(x) + x.

Grow a mask back from 1/16 size and the edges come back as blocks: a 16 × 16-pixel block is all-in or all-out.

U-Net passes each encoder map across to the decoder map of the same size (the dashed lines of slide 23), so the decoder has the fine edges again.

Worked example · measured on the photo

mask IoU with the exact mask: from 1/4 size 0.968 · from 1/16 size 0.877 — the loss is all at the edges

🇹🇭 ชี้สามภาพ: mask จริง · ขยายกลับจาก 1/4 (IoU 0.97) · จาก 1/16 (IoU 0.88 ขอบเป็นบล็อก) · skip connection ของคาบ 4 D ส่งรายละเอียดข้ามไปให้ decoder

Try it

Who took it, who is in itCLIP learned from 400 million image–text pairs from the internet (session 5, slide 34). The people in them were not asked.

D · Open vocabulary & responsibility

A detector inherits its model's bias

CLIP's own authors probed it with photos of faces (FairFace) and a list of labels that included non-human and crime words (Radford et al., 2021, §7):

  • 4.9% of the faces were labelled “animal”, “gorilla”, “chimpanzee” or “orangutan” — about 14% of images in the group labelled Black, under 8% in every other group.
  • 16.5% of men's images were put in “thief”, “suspicious person” or “criminal”, against 9.8% of women's.
  • Adding the label “child” to the list cut such labels for ages 0–2 from 30.3% to 2.3%: the list of words the designer chooses changes who is harmed.
Session 5, slide 38: CLIP counted four elephants as three and read a note as an iPod.

Open-vocabulary detectors start from image and text encoders pre-trained on web image–text pairs “similar to CLIP” (Minderer et al., 2022). What the encoders learned can come along into every box — and you choose the words.

So test with the words and the people your system will meet before anyone relies on it.

🇹🇭 ตัวเลขทั้งหมดตรวจกับ Radford et al. 2021 §7.1 แล้ว (FairFace probes ที่ผู้เขียน CLIP ทำเอง) · อ่านตรง ๆ ไม่ตีความเกิน · ประเด็นสอน: ชุดคำที่ผู้ออกแบบเลือกเปลี่ยนว่าใครถูกกระทบ (กรณี 'child') · OWL-ViT เริ่มจาก encoder แบบ CLIP (Minderer 2022) — ไม่ได้วัด OWL-ViT เอง จึงพูดว่า 'สืบทอดได้'

🇬🇧 Every number checked against Radford et al. 2021 §7.1 (the CLIP authors' own FairFace probes). Read them as they are. The lesson: the label list the designer chooses changes who is harmed (the 'child' case). OWL-ViT starts from CLIP-like encoders (Minderer 2022) — we did not measure OWL-ViT, so say 'can carry over'.

D · Open vocabulary & responsibility

Cameras that find people: who is watched, who decides?

A person detector is a surveillance toolSession 1, slide 5 showed traffic-safety cameras. CLIP's authors tested CCTV images and celebrity recognition themselves — “given its societal implications” (Radford et al., 2021, §7.2).
Consent and purposeWho agreed to be filmed? What is the system for — counting cyclists, or finding one person? Who can see the footage, and for how long is it kept?
The lawUnder Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA, B.E. 2562), images in which a person can be identified are personal data: there must be a lawful basis and a stated purpose.

Session 8 · what to remember

What, where, which pixels — and at what cost

  1. Metrics: IoU ≥ 0.5 makes a hit; precision and recall trade through the threshold; NMS keeps one box per object.
  2. Detection: one look, a grid at three strides — 8,400 guesses at 640 — then a threshold and NMS. Fine-tune the head.
  3. Segmentation: shrink, grow back with transposed convolutions, skip connections for edges; SAM masks from a click or a box.
  4. Open vocabulary: sentences as classes; the data and the words carry bias — measure by group, ask who is watched.
