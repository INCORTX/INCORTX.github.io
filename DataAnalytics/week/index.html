Every Assignment, Step by Step

The same cycle, six times. Everything you need each week is on this page.

  1. In session N

    Watch the 45-minute demo, then spend an hour on your group’s topic. Hand in the notebook as it is when the hour ends.

    QuestionEDAInsightIssueSolutionMetric
  2. At home

    Finish the notebook, make the slides, record the video — every member speaks — and write a README.

    Due two days before session N+1
  3. Start of session N+1

    Two of you present the notebook for four minutes, then answer questions.

Session 1 has a 120-minute work block and nothing to present. Assignment 6 is presented briefly at the end of session 6.

Hand in four files

  • Notebook.ipynb
  • Video, 5 minutes or more.mp4
  • Slides, 10 pages or more.pdf or .pptx
  • README — data source, what you borrowed, where you used AI.md

Files only, uploaded to Google Classroom. No links, no .zip. A late or wrongly named file counts as not handed in.
