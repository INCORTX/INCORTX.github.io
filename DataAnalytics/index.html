Data Analytics

Lab sessions for Data Analytics, DTii 1/2026 at KMITL. Six hands-on sessions running alongside the lecture course, from raw files through to models you can defend.

Everything runs in Google Colab. Nothing to install, no files to upload — every dataset in these notebooks loads in a single line.

Every session is an assignment, and they are all marked the same way

Group work, three or four people, groups named A–J. One topic per group.

45 minDemo — watch, do not type along
60 minYour hour — your topic, five steps
60 minEveryone presents, then questions

The five steps of your hour are also exactly what you present: EDA → Insight → Problem → Solution → Metric. Each one has to be opened on screen from your notebook.

Seven things are marked:

  • 01Presenting in the session
  • 02Answering questions about your code
  • 03Handing in every file on time
  • 04Video — fluent, and it analyses something
  • 05Slides — visualisation worth looking at
  • 06The homework checklist, complete
  • 07Slide count and video length
Four rules that decide marks before anything else is read
  • Everything is a file. No YouTube, Drive, Google Slides or Colab links — .ipynb, .mp4, .pdf/.pptx, README.
  • Homework is due the day before the next session.
  • Everybody presents every assignment — two of you in the room, the rest in the video.
  • You are not required to get a good result. A model that loses to its own baseline scores just as well, if you can say why.
